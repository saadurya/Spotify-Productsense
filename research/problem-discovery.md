# Spotify — Problem Discovery

## Objective

Identify potential problems faced by different college-student user segments before considering solutions.

These problems are hypotheses based on the available evidence and should not be treated as validated user problems yet.

---

## 1. Music Explorers

### User Goal
Discover new music that matches their interests.

### Potential Problem
Users may find it difficult to consistently discover music that feels genuinely relevant rather than repeatedly receiving familiar or similar recommendations.

### Why This Could Matter
Music discovery is an important part of the Spotify experience. If recommendations become repetitive or feel irrelevant, users may spend less time exploring new content.

---

## 2. Routine Listeners

### User Goal
Quickly listen to music they already enjoy.

### Potential Problem
Users may have difficulty deciding what to listen to when they want something familiar but do not want to manually search through playlists or songs.

### Why This Could Matter
Frequent listeners may value low-effort access to relevant music.

---

## 3. Social Listeners

### User Goal
Share and experience music with friends.

### Potential Problem
Discovering what friends are listening to or creating shared music experiences may require users to actively share playlists, songs, or links.

### Why This Could Matter
Music can be part of social experiences, but the value depends on how easily users can discover and participate in those experiences.

---

## 4. Context-Based Listeners

### User Goal
Find music appropriate for a specific activity, situation, or mood.

### Potential Problem
Users may spend time deciding what to play when their listening need is based on a specific context such as studying, travelling, exercising, relaxing, or attending an event.

### Why This Could Matter
The same user may want completely different music depending on what they are doing.

---

## 5. Identity & Memory Listeners

### User Goal
Use music to represent or remember meaningful experiences.

### Potential Problem
Users may create playlists around important moments, but those moments may become difficult to organize, revisit, or connect with later.

### Why This Could Matter
Spotify's published student analysis showed that students created thousands of playlists associated with graduation and that listening behaviour changed across college years.

---

# Problem Summary

| User Segment | Potential Problem |
|---|---|
| Music Explorers | Difficulty consistently discovering relevant new music |
| Routine Listeners | Difficulty quickly choosing familiar music |
| Social Listeners | Friction in discovering and participating in shared music experiences |
| Context-Based Listeners | Difficulty finding the right music for a specific context |
| Identity & Memory Listeners | Difficulty organizing and revisiting music associated with meaningful moments |

---

# Important Product Sense Note

These are **potential problems**, not confirmed problems.

Before selecting one, we need to evaluate:

1. How important is the problem?
2. How frequently does it occur?
3. How many users experience it?
4. How well is Spotify already solving it?
5. Is there an opportunity for meaningful improvement?
6. Can Spotify realistically address it?

The next step will be to compare these problems and determine which opportunity is worth investigating further.
