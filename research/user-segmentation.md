# Spotify — User Segmentation

## Broad Target User

College students who use Spotify.

However, college students are not a single homogeneous user group. Their listening behaviour and needs can differ based on how they use music in their daily lives.

---

## Initial User Segments

### 1. Music Explorers

**Description:**  
Students who actively discover new artists, songs, genres, and playlists.

**Primary Goal:**  
Discover music that matches their interests and introduces them to something new.

**Potential Needs:**
- New music discovery
- Personalized recommendations
- Exploring different genres and artists

---

### 2. Routine Listeners

**Description:**  
Students who regularly listen to a familiar set of songs, playlists, or artists.

**Primary Goal:**  
Quickly access music they already enjoy without spending much time deciding what to play.

**Potential Needs:**
- Easy access to familiar music
- Personalized playlists
- Low-effort listening

---

### 3. Social Listeners

**Description:**  
Students who use music as part of their social experiences with friends or communities.

**Primary Goal:**  
Share, discover, and experience music with other people.

**Potential Needs:**
- Sharing music
- Collaborative playlists
- Discovering music through friends
- Music-based social experiences

---

### 4. Context-Based Listeners

**Description:**  
Students who choose music based on what they are doing or how they want to feel.

Examples could include studying, exercising, travelling, relaxing, or attending a social event.

**Primary Goal:**  
Find suitable audio for a particular activity, situation, or mood.

**Potential Needs:**
- Activity-based recommendations
- Mood-based discovery
- Context-aware playlists
- Quick selection of suitable music

---

### 5. Identity & Memory Listeners

**Description:**  
Students who connect music with personal experiences, memories, relationships, or important moments.

**Primary Goal:**  
Use music to represent, remember, or share meaningful experiences.

**Potential Needs:**
- Personal playlists
- Music connected to memories
- Sharing meaningful playlists
- Capturing important moments through music

---

## Important Note

These segments are **working hypotheses**, not validated user segments.

The purpose of this exercise is to create an initial framework that can later be tested against additional evidence and user behaviour.

A single Spotify user may also belong to multiple segments depending on the situation.
