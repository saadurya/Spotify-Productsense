## 2. Student User Evidence

### Evidence #1 — Listening changes across college years

Spotify's 2025 "Best in Class" analysis used streaming data from U.S. Spotify Student plan subscribers across their college years.

The analysis showed that students' most-streamed tracks varied from freshman through senior year. Spotify also reported more than 37,000 U.S. playlists containing "graduation" or "Class of 2025" in their titles during the 2024–25 school year.

Source:
Spotify Newsroom — Best in Class (2025)

### Limitation

This evidence represents U.S. Spotify Student plan subscribers, not all college students globally.
