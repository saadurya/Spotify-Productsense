# Spotify — Existing Evidence

## 1. Product Context

Spotify is a music and audio streaming platform with a dedicated Student plan.

The Student plan is designed for eligible college/university students and provides access to Spotify Premium benefits.

For this Product Sense exercise, we are focusing specifically on college students as a target user group.

---

## 2. Student User Evidence

### Evidence #1 — Listening changes across college years

Spotify's 2025 "Best in Class" analysis used streaming data from U.S. Spotify Student plan subscribers across their college years.

The analysis showed that students' most-streamed tracks varied from freshman through senior year.

Spotify also reported more than 37,000 U.S. playlists containing "graduation" or "Class of 2025" in their titles during the 2024–25 school year.

**Source:**  
Spotify Newsroom — "Best in Class" (2025)

https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-05-01/best-in-class-college-student-listening-graduation-2025/

### Limitation

This evidence represents U.S. Spotify Student plan subscribers, not all college students globally.

---

## 3. Observed Behaviours

Based on the evidence above:

- Listening preferences can change across different stages of college.
- Students use playlists to represent or capture meaningful college moments such as graduation.
- Music can be connected to different stages and experiences during college.

These are observations from the available evidence and should not yet be treated as confirmed product problems.

---

## 4. Potential Pain Points

At this stage, we are not claiming that these are confirmed pain points.

The evidence suggests areas that could be investigated further:

- Students may have different music needs at different stages of college.
- Students may use music to reflect specific experiences, memories, or occasions.
- There may be opportunities to help students discover or organize music around important moments.

These are hypotheses, not validated problems.

---

## 5. Evidence vs Hypothesis

### Evidence

- Spotify analyzed listening behaviour of U.S. Student plan subscribers across their college years.
- Listening patterns differed across freshman, sophomore, junior, and senior years.
- Spotify reported more than 37,000 playlists containing "graduation" or "Class of 2025" in the U.S. during the 2024–25 school year.

### Hypotheses to investigate

- College students may have different music needs depending on their stage of college.
- Students may value music experiences connected to specific college moments.
- Students may have unmet needs around discovering, organizing, or sharing music for these moments.

---

## Research Limitations

The evidence currently available is limited to Spotify's published analysis of U.S. Student plan subscribers.

Therefore, we should avoid assuming that the same behaviours apply equally to:

- All college students
- Students outside the U.S.
- Non-Student-plan Spotify users

Further evidence would be needed before treating these hypotheses as representative of the broader college-student population.
